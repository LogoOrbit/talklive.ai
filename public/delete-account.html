Delete your TalkLive account

Last Updated: 5th November 2026

In the Android app

  1. Open TalkLive and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password (or type DELETE if you signed in with Google).

Without the app

Email info@talklive.app from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my TalkLive account” and your username. We confirm it is you and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, display name, email, password and Google sign-in link.
  • Your profile: avatar, friend ID, friends, friend requests, chats, voice messages, call history, streak, passport and favourite films.
  • Contact info you chose to save, and push notification tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports and moderation records about rule breaking, only as long as needed to keep people safe and prevent ban evasion (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Purchase records required for tax and accounting, as the law requires.

Messages you sent to other people may stay in their copy of the chat until they clear it. A Google Play subscription is cancelled in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions), not by deleting the account.
