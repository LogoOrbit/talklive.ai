Frequently asked questions

How do I meet new people on TalkLive?

Just tap to talk. TalkLive instantly matches you with a new person for a live voice conversation - no profiles or swiping needed.

Can I make lasting friends?

Yes. When you enjoy talking with someone, add them as a friend so you can message and call them again later.

Is it free to meet people here?

Core random matching is free. Free users wait about five seconds between calls, and optional Premium adds advanced filters and removes that wait.

Can I meet people from specific countries?

Yes. Optional filters let you focus on particular regions, or leave them off to meet people from everywhere.

Do I need to show my face?

No. TalkLive is voice-only, so you meet people through conversation, not appearance.
