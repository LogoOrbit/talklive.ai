Questions

What is the difference between Plus and a pass?

They unlock exactly the same things. Plus is $10 a month and renews until you cancel. A pass is a one-time payment for 1, 7 or 15 days and then simply ends.

How do I buy?

Open the TalkLive Android app, tap Plus and choose a plan or a pass. You pay through Google Play. Your Plus works on the website too when you sign in with the same account.

How do I cancel Plus?

In Google Play: Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, TalkLive. You keep Plus until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I buy a pass while I have Plus?

Yes. The days are added on top, so you never lose time you paid for.

Is the free plan changing?

No. Voice calls, text chats and friends stay free and unlimited.

Do calls cost anything?

TalkLive never charges for a call or a message. Your mobile plan still counts the data: a voice call uses roughly tens of megabytes an hour. See how a random call works.

Refunds

Purchases go through Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play's rules. The Refund Policy explains the details, and support is happy to help.
