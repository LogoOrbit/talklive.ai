Frequently asked questions

How do I get Plus free by inviting friends?

Share your invite link from the app. Every 3 friends who create a new, email-verified account with your link and have their first real conversation (a call of a minute or more) earn you 1 hour of TalkLive Plus. You need an account yourself, and invites from your own network or devices do not count. When the hour ends, you are back on Free and keep your streak, passport and friends.

What is "see who liked you"?

When someone taps ❤️ during your call, it is saved for 30 days. Everyone sees how many people liked them; Premium shows who they were and lets you call back anyone who is online.

When can I buy Premium?

There is no date yet. Premium is not on sale, no payment method is connected, and nothing on TalkLive charges you today.

What will it cost?

Pricing will be published on this page before Premium goes on sale. Anything you may have seen earlier was a placeholder.

Is the free plan changing?

No. Free random voice and text matching stays free, with the limits listed above.

Will Premium need an account?

No. It will be tied to the unique TalkLive ID stored in your browser (shown in Settings), the same as everything else on the site.

Do calls cost anything?

TalkLive never charges for a call or a message. Your mobile plan still counts the data: a voice call uses roughly tens of megabytes an hour, and text chat almost nothing. There is no phone number involved, so there are no call charges from your carrier. See how a random call works.

What happens if I already paid for something?

Premium is not on sale and no payment method is connected, so nothing on TalkLive can charge you. If you see a charge that mentions TalkLive, write to support; the Refund Policy explains how refunds work.
