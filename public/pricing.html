Questions

What is the difference between Plus and a pass?

They unlock exactly the same things. Plus is billed monthly or yearly, in your own currency, and renews until you cancel. A pass is a one-time payment for 1, 7 or 15 days and then simply ends. Prices are set per country by Google Play.

How do I buy?

Plus and passes will be sold only in the TalkLive Android app, through Google Play. The app is not on Google Play yet: tap "Notify me when Plus launches" and we will email you the day it is. Your Plus will work on the website too when you sign in with the same account.

How do I cancel Plus?

In Google Play: Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, TalkLive. You keep Plus until the end of the month or year you paid for.

Can I buy a pass while I have Plus?

Yes. The days are added on top, so you never lose time you paid for.

Refunds

Purchases go through Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play's rules. The Refund Policy explains the details, and support is happy to help.
