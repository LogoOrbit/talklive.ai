What Chatroulette is

Chatroulette is the site that started it. Launched in 2009, it paired two webcams at random and let either side press Next. Within months it was a worldwide curiosity, and nearly every random chat service since is a variation on it.

It is still running, still video-first, and has added moderation and filters over the years to deal with the problem it became known for.

Why people look for something else

The idea was never the issue. Meeting someone you would not otherwise meet is still the whole appeal. The camera is what people get tired of: being looked at before you have said a word, and not knowing what you will be shown when the next person appears.

Voice changes that. You are judged on what you say, the surprises are limited to an accent, and you can talk while cooking, walking or lying in the dark.

How TalkLive works

Open the site or the Android app and tap Talk for a voice call, or Chat for text. You are paired with one other adult, usually within seconds. There is no camera at any point, no account to create and no phone number to give. Calls are never recorded and go through a relay, so the other person does not see your IP address.

If the conversation is not for you, one tap moves you on. Report and Block are on every call and chat, and our safety page explains what happens when you use them. If you click with someone, add them as a friend and call or message them later without swapping numbers.

Questions people ask

Is there a Chatroulette without video?

TalkLive works the same way, one random stranger at a time with a Next button, but by voice call or text chat. There is no camera.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open TalkLive and tap Talk or Chat. There is no account, email or phone number involved.

Who is on TalkLive?

Adults from around the world, with the most people online from India, the United States, Pakistan, Bangladesh and the United Kingdom. It is for people aged 18 and over.

Are calls recorded?

No. Calls are not recorded, and they pass through a relay so the other person does not see your IP address.
