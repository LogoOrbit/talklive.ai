What OmeTV is

OmeTV is a random video chat service: you open it, allow your camera and microphone, and are paired face to face with a stranger. Swipe to move to the next person. It runs on the web and as mobile apps, and it is one of the best-known successors to the roulette-style video chat sites of the 2010s.

It is built around being seen. The camera is the product.

Why people look for something else

Most people who search for an alternative are not unhappy with random chat. They are tired of the camera. Video means checking how you look, where you are sitting and what is behind you, and it means seeing whatever the other person chooses to show you the moment the call connects.

Others want something that works on a weak connection, since video needs far more data than audio, or do not want to sign in with a social account just to talk to someone for five minutes.

How TalkLive works

Open the site or the Android app and tap Talk for a voice call, or Chat for text. You are paired with one other adult, usually within seconds. There is no camera at any point, no account to create and no phone number to give. Calls are never recorded and go through a relay, so the other person does not see your IP address.

If the conversation is not for you, one tap moves you on. Report and Block are on every call and chat, and our safety page explains what happens when you use them. If you click with someone, add them as a friend and call or message them later without swapping numbers.

Questions people ask

Is there an app like OmeTV without video?

Yes. TalkLive matches you with a random person for a voice call or a text chat. There is no camera at any point.

Do I need an account?

No. Open TalkLive, tap Talk or Chat and you are matched. An account is optional and only needed to keep friends across devices.

Is TalkLive free?

Yes. Voice calls and text chats are free and unlimited. An optional Plus plan adds extra filters and cosmetic perks.

Can the other person see my IP address?

No. Calls on TalkLive go through a relay, so neither side learns the other one's IP address.
