Questions, answered

Is TalkLive really free?

Yes. Random voice calls and text chats are free, with no sign-up. A few ads keep it running; an optional Premium plan for extra filters is on the way.

Do I need an account or a camera?

No. Tap once and you are matched. There is no video at all - just your voice or your keyboard - and no real name, email or phone number is needed.

Are calls recorded?

No. Voice calls run peer-to-peer between the two browsers and are never recorded or stored by TalkLive.

What if someone is rude?

Tap Next to leave instantly, or report and block them in one tap. Reports are reviewed, repeat offenders are banned automatically, and TalkLive is strictly 18+.
