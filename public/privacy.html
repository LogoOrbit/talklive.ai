Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 5th July 2026 · Last Updated: 23rd September 2026

The short version

  • Voice calls are never recorded. Audio goes between you and the other person and is not stored. On desktop browsers, automated safety checks run on your own microphone during a call to detect abuse, and only a short text snippet is kept when one is triggered - see section 4.
  • Typed messages are kept for a limited, rolling period so reports of abuse can be reviewed, then deleted automatically.
  • Gender is self-declared. It is whatever you pick under “I am” in Settings (or “Prefer not to say”), and it is used only to apply gender filters. It is never verified, never shown to the stranger during a call, and TalkLive does not analyse your voice to estimate your gender or age.
  • No account is needed. If you create one, we keep your username, a hashed password and an optional recovery email.
  • TalkLive is supported by ads. Ads come from Adsterra and are described in section 8.

The full policy below is what governs; this summary is only there so the important parts are not buried.

Voice audio is never recorded or stored; the automated safety checks described in section 4 are the only processing of what is said in a call.

4. Audio Communications

TalkLive uses WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) technology to establish live audio conversations between users. TalkLive does not record, store, archive, or retain live audio. Audio is encrypted in transit and travels between users either directly or through a TURN relay that forwards the encrypted stream without being able to decode it; no person at TalkLive listens to calls, and live conversations are not stored by TalkLive after they end.

Automated safety checks during voice calls. On desktop browsers that support it, TalkLive runs automated safety checks on your own microphone while a call is connected, to detect abuse. Your browser's built-in speech recognition turns what you say into text (depending on the browser, this may be processed by the browser's provider - for example Google, for Chrome), and that text is checked for abusive words and sustained hostile language; the volume level is also checked for sustained shouting. These checks do not run on phones and tablets. Audio is never recorded or stored, and the running text is discarded continuously. Only when a check is triggered does TalkLive keep an alert: the type of trigger, up to 500 characters of the text that triggered it, your username and country, and the username of the person you were talking to. Alerts are stored with the same limited, rolling retention as text chats, are sent to the site operator for moderation review, and are never used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Because internet communications involve third-party networks and technologies, no online communication can be guaranteed to be completely private or completely secure. Users should exercise caution when communicating with strangers online.

5. Text Chat

TalkLive provides text messaging features between users. Text messages are processed to deliver conversations between participants, and are kept on a rolling basis - for a limited period, with older messages automatically deleted as new ones arrive - so the site operator can review them for safety and moderation purposes, such as investigating reports of abuse, spam, harassment, or scams. TalkLive does not sell users' private conversations. Voice calls are never recorded or listened to by a person; apart from the automated safety alerts described in section 4, this review applies only to typed text chat.
