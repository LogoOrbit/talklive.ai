How TalkLive Works

TalkLive connects you with a random stranger for a live, audio-only conversation. There's no sign-up - tap "Start," allow microphone access, and you'll be paired with someone automatically. Tap "Next" any time to end the current call and get matched with someone new, or "Hang Up" to leave entirely. You can optionally set a gender preference, a country, and interest tags from the settings menu to help find a better match.

Quick Tips

  • Use headphones to avoid echo/feedback for both you and your match.
  • You can mute your mic at any time with the microphone button.
  • Text chat is available alongside audio via the chat icon.
  • If a call feels unsafe or inappropriate, tap "Report" - this immediately ends the call and blocks that person from being matched with you again.

Community Guidelines - Be a Good Stranger

TalkLive only works if people treat each other with basic respect. Please:

  • Be kind. Treat the person on the other end the way you'd want to be treated.
  • Keep things audio-only and consensual - don't pressure others into topics, content, or behavior they haven't agreed to.
  • Respect a "no" or a hang-up. If someone ends the call or asks you to stop, let it go.
  • Assume you're talking to a real stranger, possibly a minor despite the age requirement below - act accordingly.
  • Use the Report button if something feels wrong. Reporting helps keep this usable for everyone.

Privacy Note

A basic match does not require an account, email, or phone number. Voice uses encrypted WebRTC through a production TURN relay and is not recorded or stored by TalkLive; automated safety checks run on your own microphone during calls (speech-to-text on desktop browsers only) and keep a short record only when abuse is detected. Typed messages, reports and related context may be retained for a limited rolling period for delivery, safety and moderation. TalkLive also processes IP address, approximate location and operational information as described in the full Privacy Policy, Gender is only what you choose in Settings: it is used for gender filters, is never verified, and TalkLive does not analyse voices to estimate it.

Free random voice & text chat with strangers, worldwide

TalkLive is a browser-based random chat service for adults. Tap to Talk searches for a live voice match; Tap to Chat searches for a text match without microphone permission. Core matching is free and needs no install or account. Availability, identity, country and language are not guaranteed, so keep personal information private and leave any conversation that feels wrong.

Why people choose TalkLive

  • Two ways to connect - Tap to Talk for a live voice call, or Tap to Chat for anonymous text chat with no mic at all.
  • One-tap random matching - press either button to join the live queue; wait time depends on availability and your preferences.
  • Private by default - no real name, email, or phone number is required for a basic match. Operational data is handled under the Privacy Policy.
  • No camera, ever - no video pressure, low bandwidth, and real connection through voice or words.
  • Light enough for any phone - text chat runs smoothly even on 1GB devices and slow networks.
  • Next Stranger - not clicking? Skip to a new person in a single tap.
  • Worldwide reach - talk to people across dozens of countries, or filter to the regions you prefer.
  • Safety controls - intended for adults, with leave, block and report tools. No stranger-chat service can guarantee safety.
  • 17 languages - the whole app is translated, including right-to-left Arabic, Persian and Urdu.

How TalkLive works

TalkLive uses encrypted WebRTC audio through a production TURN relay. TalkLive does not record or store voice audio, although another participant can record on their own device. Typed messages and related context may be retained for a limited rolling period under the Privacy Policy. If both people choose, the optional friends feature can help them reconnect without exchanging phone numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Is TalkLive free? Core random voice and text matching is free, supported by a few ads - never a paywall. Wait time depends on compatible people in the live queue; optional Premium adds extra controls.

Do I need an account? No. A basic random match is account-free; an optional account keeps friends and history between visits.

Is it anonymous? A basic match needs no real name or phone number, but TalkLive processes operational data and another participant may record on their own device.

Can I chat without talking? Yes. Tap to Chat uses text without microphone permission. Typed messages may be retained under the Privacy Policy.

Is there video? No. TalkLive is intentionally voice-and-text only for privacy and comfort.

Which languages are supported? TalkLive is available in 17 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. Right-to-left languages (Arabic, Persian and Urdu) are fully supported.
