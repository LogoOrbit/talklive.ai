What Emerald Chat is

Emerald Chat is a random chat site with text, video and group rooms. It asks you to sign in, lets you match by interests, and uses a reputation system so that people who behave well are matched with each other.

It is one of the more thoughtful Omegle successors, and the comparison here is between two takes on the same goal.

Why people look for something else

The usual reasons are the sign-in step and the camera. Some people want to be talking within ten seconds of opening a page, without creating anything. Others want voice, which Emerald's one-to-one mode does not centre on: it is text or video.

TalkLive sits in the gap between them. More human than typing, less exposed than video.

How TalkLive works

Open the site or the Android app and tap Talk for a voice call, or Chat for text. You are paired with one other adult, usually within seconds. There is no camera at any point, no account to create and no phone number to give. Calls are never recorded and go through a relay, so the other person does not see your IP address.

If the conversation is not for you, one tap moves you on. Report and Block are on every call and chat, and our safety page explains what happens when you use them. If you click with someone, add them as a friend and call or message them later without swapping numbers.

Questions people ask

Is there a site like Emerald Chat without login?

TalkLive needs no login. Open it, tap Talk or Chat and you are matched with one person.

Does TalkLive have voice chat?

Yes. Voice calls are the main way people use it, with text chat as the other option. There is no video.

Can I choose who I am matched with?

You can prefer countries and add interests for free. Plus adds more filters.

How is bad behaviour handled?

Every call and chat has Report and Block, automated checks watch for spam and scripted accounts, and people who are reported repeatedly are warned and then removed.

Free, no sign-up, adults only. Someone is online now.
