About TalkLive

TalkLive is a voice-first social platform that connects you with someone new somewhere in the world for a live, private voice conversation. One tap, no sign-up, no video - just a real human voice on the other end of the line.

Our Mission

The internet made it easy to scroll past millions of people and hard to actually talk to one. TalkLive exists to bring back spontaneous, genuine conversation: the kind you'd have with a stranger on a train, except the train spans the whole planet. We believe voice - without profiles, photos, or pressure - is the most honest way for two strangers to meet.

New here? Read What is TalkLive? for a visual tour.

What Makes TalkLive Different

  • Audio only. No camera anxiety, no appearance judgments. You connect through what you say, not how you look.
  • Private by design. No real name, email, or phone number is required. You appear as a display name you choose.
  • Private calls. Voice uses encrypted WebRTC through a production TURN relay. TalkLive does not record or store call audio.
  • Real safety tools. Instant report and block, automatic bans for repeat offenders, and a strict 18+ policy.
  • Friends, not followers. When a conversation clicks, add each other as friends and call back later - no phone numbers exchanged.
  • Truly global. A 12-language interface with right-to-left support, and people online from dozens of countries at any moment.

How It Works

Tap the green button, allow microphone access, and the matching service searches for an available person using optional preferences. Voice uses encrypted WebRTC through a production TURN relay, and TalkLive does not record or store call audio. Tap Next or Hang Up any time to leave.

Free and Premium

TalkLive's core random voice and text matching is free. TalkLive Premium is coming soon and will add advanced filters, more friend capacity, no between-call wait and an ad-free experience. It is not available for purchase yet.
