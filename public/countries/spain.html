The country that invented sobremesa

Spanish has a word for staying at the table long after the food is gone, just to keep talking: sobremesa. It can last longer than the meal. That tells you most of what you need to know about talking to people here. Conversation is not a way to pass the time, it is the plan.

People talk fast, interrupt warmly and stay up late. Dinner is at nine or ten, so the best hours to find someone are from early evening until well past midnight.

Carta de palabras

¿Qué tal?
How are you? The answer is "bien, ¿y tú?" every time.
Vale
OK, sure, got it. You will hear it ten times a minute.
Tío / tía
Mate, dude. Literally uncle and aunt.
Guay
Cool. "Qué guay" covers most good news.
Mola
I like it. "Mola mucho" means it is great.
Venga
Come on, right then, bye. Ends every phone call.
Madre mía
Oh my. Surprise, shock, or the electricity bill.
No pasa nada
No worries. The national philosophy.

Use tú with almost everyone; usted is for grandparents and paperwork. And remember that ahora means now, while ahora mismo means actually now.

Tortilla: con cebolla o sin cebollaWith onion or without. The country is split, families have fallen out, and it is the safest way to start an argument with a stranger. Pick a side before you call.

What to talk about

Football, and whether you are Madrid, Barça, Atleti or loyal to somewhere smaller. Local fiestas, because every town has one and swears it is the best. Where to eat, which region does it right, the heat in August and plans for the next puente. Ask someone about their pueblo and you will get a full tour.

Spain has four official languages, so do not be surprised by a "bon dia", a "kaixo" or a "bos días".

Stay safe

Keep your full name, address, workplace and social accounts to yourself at first. Never share a Bizum request, bank code or verification code, and end the call with anyone asking for money, photos or to move to another app straight away. Preguntas frecuentes

¿TalkLive es gratis en España?

Sí. Voice calls and text chat are free, with no account, no phone number and no camera. Choosing Spain as a preferred country is free too.

Can I chat in Spanish?

Yes. Speak any language you and your match share. Most people from Spain chat in Spanish, and you will also meet Catalan, Galician and Basque speakers.

Can I practise my Spanish here?

Yes. Say you are learning at the start and ask your match to keep going in Spanish. A real conversation is the fastest way to stop translating in your head.

When is Spain online on TalkLive?

TalkLive is busiest between 15:00 and 21:00 UTC, which is 17:00 to 23:00 in Spain in summer and 16:00 to 22:00 in winter. Spain stays up late, so evenings run long.

What is the emergency number in Spain?

Call 112 for police, ambulance or fire, free from any phone. The Policía Nacional is 091.
