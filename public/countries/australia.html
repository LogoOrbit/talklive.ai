A big country, a lot of quiet nights

Australia is huge and spread out, and plenty of people end up with an empty arvo or a long night shift and nobody to chat to. TalkLive fixes that in one tap. You get a real person on a voice call, or switch to text if you are on the train or the housemates are asleep.

Because of the time zones, Australia is a bit special. Here is when it is good to jump on:

7pm to 11pm AEDTEvening for you, afternoon in India and Pakistan, morning in the UK. Great mix of people.
Weekend arvosLazy Saturday and Sunday afternoons, when everyone else in the world is having their Friday or Saturday night.

Gen Z bonus: if something is mid it is mid everywhere, but in Australia it is also probably a bit cooked. If someone is cooked, they are done for. And yes, people really say no worries about absolutely everything.

What to talk about

Footy, but check which code first, because AFL people and NRL people are not the same people. Cricket in summer. The Bunnings sausage sizzle, and whether the onion goes under or on top. Beach versus pool, the cost of rent, uni, travel, and of course the wildlife, which non-Aussies will always ask about.

Chatting with someone overseas? People love hearing about Australia, so you will rarely be stuck for something to say.

Stay safe

Keep your full name, address, socials and workplace to yourself at first. Never share bank details or a verification code, and if someone asks for money, gift cards, photos or wants to move to another app fast, that is suss, so leave. Our scam guide covers the common tricks. Every call has Report and Block, and TalkLive is 18+ only.

Emergency: call 000 (112 also works from mobiles). Crisis support: Lifeline 13 11 14, any time.

Questions, mate

Is TalkLive free in Australia?

Yes. Voice and text chat are free, with no account, no phone number and no camera. Setting Australia as a preferred country is free as well.

Will I get matched with other Australians?

Sometimes. You can prefer Australia in Filters, but matching depends on who is online. Global matching is the fastest and usually means people from India, the UK, the US and beyond.

What is the best time to chat from Australia?

Aussie evenings, roughly 7 pm to 11 pm on the east coast, line up with afternoon in South Asia and morning in Europe, so there is always someone around. TalkLive is busiest globally between 15:00 and 21:00 UTC, which is the middle of the night in Australia.

Do I need to download an app?

Nah. It runs in your browser on Android, iPhone and computer. Add it to your home screen if you want an icon.

What is the emergency number in Australia?

Call 000 for police, ambulance or fire. 112 also works from mobile phones. For crisis support, Lifeline is 13 11 14.

Keen for a yarn?

Someone, somewhere is up for a chat. Could be in Adelaide, could be in Mumbai. Only one way to find out.
