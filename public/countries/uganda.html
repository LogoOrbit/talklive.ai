A country that greets properly

In Uganda you do not open with your question. You greet first, ask how the person is, how home is, and only then get to the point. That habit makes Ugandans very good at talking to strangers: nobody is in a rush, and nobody minds a long hello.

It is also one of the youngest countries in the world, with most people under 25, so expect talk of school, hustle, music and plans.

Greet like you mean it

  • Oli otya?GyendiLuganda: "How are you?" - "I am fine."
  • Gyebale koKale"Well done" - said as a hello to anyone busy. "OK, thanks."
  • Wasuze otya?BulungiMorning: "How was your night?" - "Good."
  • Habari?NzuriKiswahili, understood across East Africa.

Ssebo is sir and nnyabo is madam, and adding one makes any greeting polite. Webale is thank you. "You are lost!" just means "I haven't seen you in a while", and "well done" is said to anyone doing anything at all.

Not that kind of RolexHere a Rolex is rolled eggs: an omelette wrapped in a hot chapati, sold on the street corner. Ask anyone where the best one is and you have a ten-minute conversation.

What to talk about

The Uganda Cranes, and which English Premier League club is yours, because everyone has one. Music, from kadongo kamu to the latest Kampala hits. Boda-boda stories, matoke versus posho, weekend plans in Jinja, and which region has the best food. Ask where someone grew up and you will learn a language, a dish and a greeting.

Stay safe

Keep your full name, address, workplace and social accounts to yourself at first. Never share a Mobile Money PIN or a code sent to your phone, and end the call with anyone who asks for money, airtime, photos or to move to another app straight away. Questions people ask

Is TalkLive free in Uganda?

Yes. Voice calls and text chat are free, with no account, no phone number and no camera. Choosing Uganda as a preferred country is free as well.

How much data does a voice call use?

A voice call uses roughly 0.3 to 0.5 MB a minute, far less than video. Text chat uses almost none, so it suits a small data bundle.

Which languages can I use?

Any language you and your match share. Most people from Uganda chat in English, and many also speak Luganda, Kiswahili, Runyankole, Acholi or Lusoga.

When is Uganda online on TalkLive?

TalkLive is busiest between 15:00 and 21:00 UTC, which is 6 in the evening to midnight in Uganda (East Africa Time, UTC+3).

What are the emergency numbers in Uganda?

Call 999 or 112 for the police. Both are free from any phone.
