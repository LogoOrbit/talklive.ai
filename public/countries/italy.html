Parla come un italiano

The Italian you learn in school is lovely. The Italian people actually speak on a call is something else. Here is your survival kit.

RagaGuys. How every group chat message in Italy starts.

Bella!Hi, bye, nice, all in one word. Bella zio, bella frà.

BohNo idea. Comes with a shrug, always.

Che ansiaThis is stressing me out. Used for exams and unanswered texts.

Ci staFair enough, makes sense, I’m in.

Che sbattiWhat a hassle. The Monday morning word.

Una figataSomething seriously cool.

TopPerfect. Yes, the English word, said very Italian.

Gen Z bonus: things that are a lot are un botto, being dramatic is fare il drama, and if you say spoilerare everyone knows exactly what you mean. And yes, hand gestures count even on a voice call. People can hear them.

What to talk about

Calcio is the easy door. Ask someone if they are Inter, Milan, Juve, Roma or Napoli and settle in. Food is the other one, but be careful: pineapple on pizza, cream in carbonara and breaking spaghetti are all fighting words. Sanremo, summer by the sea and which city has the best coffee will keep you going for hours.

Learning Italian? Say so. People love helping, and a call fixes your pronunciation faster than any app. The site also works in italiano.

Stay safe

Keep your full name, address, Instagram and workplace private at first. Never share bank codes or SMS codes, and say ciao to anyone asking for money, photos or a quick move to another app. Our scam guide explains the classic tricks. Every call and chat has Report and Block, and TalkLive is 18+ only.

Emergenza: call 112, free from any phone, anywhere in Italy.
