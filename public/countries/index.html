How to use this properly

Picking one country narrows the pool, which means slower matches and a smaller range of people - worth it when you are practising a language or genuinely curious about a place, not worth it at 3am when the country you picked is asleep. Selecting three or four related countries is usually the better move: still targeted, still fast.

If your goal is the language rather than the place, the languages hub is the page you actually want - a language spans many countries and the two intents are not the same.

Cities

Matching is country-level, not city-level, but people search by city constantly and the cities hub covers the 113 that come up most - what makes each one distinct and which country filter reaches it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose which country I get matched with?

Yes, and it is free. Add one or more countries to your preferred list in match filters. If nobody from those countries is available quickly, TalkLive widens the search rather than leaving you waiting.

Does filtering by country reveal where I am?

No. Your own country is shown to a match only as a country name and flag - never a city, address or IP. Choosing who to talk to reveals nothing extra about you.

Why is a country I picked so quiet?

Almost always timezone. Every country page here lists the local peak hours; a country in the middle of its night has very few people online no matter how large it is.

Is there a limit on how many countries I can pick?

The free tier covers a handful of countries per list, which is enough for almost everyone. TalkLive Plus lifts the cap if you want to select a whole region.

Which countries are busiest overall?

India, Pakistan, the United States, Brazil, Indonesia and the Philippines carry the most traffic, but "busiest" depends entirely on when you are looking - each has a different evening.
