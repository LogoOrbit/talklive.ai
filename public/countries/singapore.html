Like a hawker centre, but for conversation

Singapore’s hawker centres are the best kind of chaos. Everyone sits together, strangers share tables, and the food is always worth the queue. TalkLive is the same idea for talking. You sit down, you get matched with someone new, you chat as long as it is shiok, then you move to the next stall.

Fun fact for your next call: Singapore’s hawker culture is on the UNESCO list of the world’s intangible cultural heritage. Yes, the chicken rice debate is officially heritage now.

What to talk about

Food, obviously. Best chicken rice, best laksa, whether bubble tea is a meal. The weather, which is hot, or raining, or both. NS stories, uni life, the MRT at peak hour, the National Day Parade and the songs everyone secretly knows. People overseas are super curious about Singapore, so you will never run out of things to say.

Multilingual? Show it off. A lot of people on TalkLive are practising English, Mandarin or Malay and would love a patient partner.

Stay safe

Keep your full name, address, socials and workplace private at first. Never share your Singpass, bank OTP or verification code with anyone. Got questions ah?

Is TalkLive free in Singapore?

Yes. Voice and text chat are free, no account, no phone number, no camera. Choosing Singapore as a preferred country is free too.

Can I talk in Singlish, Mandarin, Malay or Tamil?

Sure. Talk in whatever language you and your match share. Most people will happily mix English with a bit of everything, just like at home.

What is the best time to chat from Singapore?

Evenings from about 7 pm to midnight are good, because they line up with evening in India and Pakistan and afternoon in Europe. TalkLive is busiest globally from 15:00 to 21:00 UTC, which is 11 pm to 5 am in Singapore.

Do I need to download an app?

No need. TalkLive runs in the browser on Android, iPhone and computer, and you can add it to your home screen.

What is the emergency number in Singapore?

Call 999 for police, or 995 for an ambulance or the fire service. Both are free from any phone.
