Pull up a stool

Life in Vietnam happens on the pavement, on low plastic stools, over iced coffee or a bowl of something hot. Strangers share tables, ask where you are from and how old you are within a minute, and nobody finds it rude. It is how you work out what to call each other.

It is also a young country that is keen to practise English, so a lot of people open the app for exactly that. Offer ten minutes of English and you will get ten minutes of Vietnamese back.

First, who are you talking to?

Vietnamese has no single word for "you". You pick one by age, which is why the age question comes so early:

AnhA man a little older than you
ChịA woman a little older than you
EmAnyone younger than you
BạnFriend. The safe choice with someone your age
Xin chàoHello. Polite and safe everywhere.
Bạn khỏe không?How are you? Answer: "Tôi khỏe, cảm ơn."
Ăn cơm chưa?Have you eaten yet? It means "how are you doing".
Cảm ơnThank you.
Trời ơi!Oh heavens! For surprise, traffic and prices.
Một, hai, ba, dô!One, two, three, cheers!
Cà phê sữa đáStrong dark coffee dripped through a metal phin onto condensed milk, poured over ice. Ask anyone where the best one is in their city. You will be there a while.

What to talk about

Food, always: phở in the north against the south, bún chả, bánh mì, and what your match had for lunch. Football, where the national team stops traffic when it wins. Tết and the trip home, motorbike life, the weather in Hà Nội versus Sài Gòn, music and K-dramas. Ask for a food recommendation and take notes.

Stay safe

Keep your full name, address, workplace and social accounts to yourself at first. Never share a bank OTP or a login code, and end the call with anyone asking for money, photos or to move to Zalo or another app straight away. Our scam guide lists the usual tricks, and every call and chat has Report and Block.

Can I chat in Vietnamese?

Yes. Speak any language you and your match share. Most people from Vietnam chat in Vietnamese, and many want to practise English.

Can I practise speaking English here?

Yes. Add the English practice interest before you start and you are matched first with others who want to practise. A voice call is the quickest way to get past the fear of speaking.

When is Vietnam online on TalkLive?

Vietnam is UTC+7. Evenings from about 20:00 to midnight local time are the easiest hours to find people from Vietnam and nearby countries, and TalkLive as a whole is busiest from 22:00 Vietnam time.

What are the emergency numbers in Vietnam?

Police is 113, fire is 114 and ambulance is 115.
