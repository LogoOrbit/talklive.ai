Direct, friendly and always up for a chat

Dutch people are famous for being direct. Ask a question and you get a real answer, no fluff. That makes for honestly great conversations with strangers. Add the national obsession with gezelligheid, that warm, cosy, everyone-together feeling, and you get people who are easy to talk to and fun to listen to.

The best time to find someone is after dinner, roughly 17:00 to 23:00, when TalkLive is busiest everywhere.

Praat als een local

A few words that will make any Dutch person go "haha, nice":

GezelligCosy, fun, warm, nice. Untranslatable and used every day.
LekkerTasty, but also good, nice, comfy. Lekker bezig = doing great.
ChillSame as English, said with a hard Dutch "ch".
WollahI swear, for real. Big in Amsterdam and Rotterdam slang.
MattieMate, bro. Your mattie always has your back.
DoekoeMoney. As in: geen doekoe, no cash.
SkeerBroke. Usually right before payday.
KapotBroken, or super. Kapot lekker = insanely good.

Bonus: doei means bye, dat is echt ziek means that’s really cool (not sick), and if someone says het is wat het is, the conversation about the weather is officially over.

What to talk about

Koningsdag, when the whole country turns orange. Oranje football and every heartbreaking tournament. Borrel plans, stroopwafels versus every other cookie, and how anyone can be expected to cycle in that wind. Ask someone about their city and they will tell you, honestly, what is good and what is not.

Learning Dutch? Say it at the start, and ask your match to keep going in Dutch even when they want to switch to English. They will want to. Hold your ground.

Stay safe

Keep your full name, address, socials and workplace to yourself at first. Never share a bank code or verification code, and skip anyone asking for money, Tikkies, photos or to move to another app right away. Our scam guide lists the usual tricks. Veelgestelde vragen

Is TalkLive gratis in Nederland?

Ja. Voice and text chat are free, with no account, no phone number and no camera. Picking the Netherlands as a preferred country is free too.

Can I chat in Dutch?

Yes. Talk in any language you and your match share. Plenty of people chat in Dutch, and almost everyone switches to English easily.

When is the Netherlands busiest on TalkLive?

TalkLive peaks between 15:00 and 21:00 UTC, which is about 17:00 to 23:00 Dutch time in summer and 16:00 to 22:00 in winter.

Do I need to download an app?

Nee. TalkLive runs in the browser on Android, iPhone and computer, and you can pin it to your home screen.

What is the emergency number in the Netherlands?

Call 112 for police, ambulance or fire. For non-urgent police matters, the number is 0900-8844.
