The French art of the long chat

France is a country that loves to talk. Dinners last three hours, the apéro is basically a national sport, and a café terrace is where people go to debate everything from football to the best boulangerie on the street. If you have ever wanted to discuter avec des inconnus without the awkward small talk, French people are honestly great at it.

On TalkLive you get the same vibe, just online. One tap and you are on a call with a real person. Can’t talk out loud? Tap to Chat and type instead.

Le petit lexique

Want to sound like a local and not like a textbook? Here is the slang you will actually hear on a call or see in a chat. Use it and watch someone go "attends, t’es français ?"

WeshHey, what’s up. Works for literally everyone under 35.
TktShort for t’inquiète, no worries. You will see it in every chat.
Mdr / ptdrThe French LOL. Ptdr means you are actually dying.
AskipApparently. For gossip you cannot confirm.
J’ai le seumI’m gutted. Usually after a football result.
La flemmeThat deep, honest laziness. Ça passe crème if you admit it.
En modeLike, in the mood of. En mode chill, en mode détente.
C’est chaudIt’s tricky, or it’s amazing. Context decides.

Gen Z bonus: something amazing is giga or de ouf, something awkward is cringe (yes, same word), and if someone is en PLS they are emotionally wrecked, usually for no serious reason.

What to talk about

Food, always. Ask someone where to find the best croissant and you will get a passionate answer and maybe a feud. Football is safe too, as long as you are ready for the PSG vs OM debate, Le Classique, which nobody has ever settled. Music works, from French rap to the old classics everyone’s parents play.

If you are learning French, say it upfront. People are much kinder about your accent than you think, and a voice call is the fastest way to stop sounding like an audiobook. The whole site also works en français.

Keep it safe

Keep your full name, address, Insta and workplace to yourself at first. Never share a bank code or an SMS code, and skip anyone who asks for money, photos or wants to move to another app right away. Our scam guide covers the usual tricks. Every call has Report and Block, and TalkLive is for adults 18+.

Urgence : call 112, or 15 for medical help, 17 for police and 18 for fire. Free from any phone.

Questions fréquentes

Est-ce que TalkLive est gratuit en France ?

Oui. Voice and text chat are free, with no account, no phone number and no camera. Picking France as a preferred country is free too.

Can I chat in French?

Yes. Talk in whatever language you and your match share, and switch the whole site to French at talklive.app/fr/ if you like.

When is France busiest on TalkLive?

TalkLive is busiest between 15:00 and 21:00 UTC, which is about 17h to 23h in France in summer and 16h to 22h in winter.

Do I need to download an app?

Non. It runs in the browser on Android, iPhone and computer. Add it to your home screen if you want an icon.

What is the emergency number in France?

112 works everywhere in the EU. In France you can also call 15 for medical help (SAMU), 17 for police and 18 for the fire brigade.
