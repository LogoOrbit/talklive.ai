Edit `src/posts.html`, then re-render with:

```bash
NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node marketing/social-1080/src/render.js
```

Put **talklive.app** in your bio / link-in-bio. On Facebook, X, LinkedIn and
Pinterest, paste the link in the post itself (Instagram captions can't link).

## Posting plan (4 weeks)

- **Frequency:** 1 feed post/day + reshare to Stories with a link sticker.
- **Time:** 8–11pm in your target audience's time zone — that's when the queue is busiest, so new visitors match instantly.
- **Engagement posts first** (p03, p07, p10, p12, p09): they get comments, which pushes reach. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- **Save-able posts** (p08, p16, p06): ask people to save; saves boost reach on Instagram.
- Turn the best performers into Reels (add `MusicForTalkLive.mp3`, slow zoom) — Reels get far more non-follower reach than images.

## Platforms that also help Google ranking

These pages get indexed by Google and send backlinks or referral traffic:

1. **Pinterest** — every pin links to talklive.app and pins rank in Google Images. Post all 18, titles like "Omegle alternative – voice chat with strangers".
2. **YouTube Shorts** — Shorts appear in Google results. Upload the `TalkLive-Ad-*.mp4` files with keyword titles ("Talk to strangers online free").
3. **Reddit** — Google ranks Reddit threads high. Use the posts in `POST-KIT.md`, value first, follow each subreddit's rules.
4. **Quora** — answer "best Omegle alternative", "how to practice English speaking", "where to talk to someone at night"; link once per answer.
5. **AlternativeTo** — list TalkLive as an alternative to Omegle, Chatroulette, OmeTV. That page ranks for "Omegle alternative" searches.
6. **Product Hunt** — launch page is indexed and gives a strong backlink.
7. **Medium / Dev.to / Hashnode / Indie Hackers** — republish blog posts (set canonical to talklive.app) and the builder story.
8. **LinkedIn articles** — founder story; LinkedIn pages index fast.
9. **TikTok** — Google now indexes TikTok videos; reuse the Reels.
10. **Threads, Bluesky, X** — reshare images; quick indexing, good for brand searches.
11. **Tumblr / Flipboard** — cheap extra indexed links to blog posts.
12. **Directories:** SaaSHub, Slant, BetaList, StartupBase, Crunchbase — one-time listings with backlinks.
