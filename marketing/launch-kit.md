# TalkLive growth launch kit

## Goal

Grow real returning users and Premium conversions. Do not buy bot traffic, run traffic exchanges, or reward users for opening Adsterra links. Adsterra classifies incentivized traffic as prohibited.

## The offer

**TalkLive is anonymous voice or text chat without a camera, download, phone number, or required account.**

Primary free CTA: `https://talklive.app/?utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=voice_launch`

English-practice CTA: `https://talklive.app/practice-english-speaking?utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=english_practice`

Pakistan CTA: `https://talklive.app/pakistani-chat?utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=pakistan_launch`

Replace `CHANNEL` with `reddit`, `youtube`, `tiktok`, `instagram`, `facebook`, or `x`. The owner dashboard records these sources.

## Weekly operating cadence

1. Publish three short demonstrations showing the actual product, not generic stock footage.
2. Post one useful answer in a language-learning or friendship community before mentioning TalkLive where promotion is permitted.
3. Refresh one strong article using real user questions from Search Console; do not mass-produce thin pages.
4. Check owner-dashboard counters: acquisition source, visits, matches, 30-second calls, shares, invite arrivals, pricing views, and checkout clicks.
5. Keep a channel only when it produces matches or checkout clicks—not merely page views.

## Revenue scoreboard

Track this funnel every seven days:

`acquisition visit → search → match → 30-second call → share → invite arrival`

and

`pricing view → Patreon checkout click → paid member`

Ad impressions are a secondary metric. At the current Adsterra eCPM, improving retention and Premium conversion is worth far more than adding another banner.
