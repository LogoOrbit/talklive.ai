## 6. Push notification / email copy (when you send them)

- Streak at risk (evening): **"🔥 Your 5-day streak ends at midnight. One call keeps it."**
- New likes: **"❤️ 2 people liked your calls this week. See who →"**
- Weekly recap (Monday): **"📊 Last week: 12 calls · 7 countries · longest 18 min. Beat it?"**
- Trial ending (hour 20): **"💎 4 hours of Premium left. Try Undo skip before it's gone."**
- Win-back (7 days idle): **"🌍 Your passport has 9 stamps. Somewhere it's daytime, and someone's waiting."**

Keep it to at most one push a day, and never between 11pm and 8am local.

## 7. Where it shows up in the app (already live)

- **Home screen:** streak / passport / likes tiles, today's question, a one-time "New:" banner, then the weekly recap every Monday.
- **After a real call:** "🔥 5-day streak! · 🇰🇷 New stamp: South Korea (6 countries)".
- **First call of the day:** today's question pops up as an opener.
- **After hanging up:** "↩ Undo skip" for 15 seconds (free users see 💎 and the Premium card).
- **Premium card:** headline matches what was tapped (likes, undo, countries, gender).
- **3rd real conversation:** "Premium is yours for a day 🎉".
- **Pricing page:** new Free and Premium lists, plus trial and likes FAQs.
